Questions firms ask us.

What is AEO, and how is it different from SEO?

SEO gets your pages ranked in search results. AEO gets your firm quoted or named inside the answer itself, in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity. The two overlap: answer engines mostly cite pages that search engines already index and trust, so AEO builds on SEO rather than replacing it.

Why does a Hong Kong firm need AEO now?

Many clients now ask an AI assistant before they search or call. When someone asks which firm to use, the assistant names only a few. If yours isn’t one of them, you aren’t on the shortlist, however good your website looks.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT or Google will cite my firm?

No. No agency controls what search engines or AI platforms show. What we can do is remove the reasons they pass you over: pages they can’t read, answers they can’t quote, and a firm the rest of the web doesn’t mention. Scope and price are agreed before we start.

What is the difference between the free consultation and the Diagnostic?

In the free consultation we check your firm in Google, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity and walk you through what we find. The Diagnostic goes further: it compares your site with two local competitors in Google and in AI search. It starts from HK$1,500, and the fee is credited toward a rebuild.

Do I need separate English and Chinese pages?

Yes, if your clients search in both languages. AI tools answer in the language of the question and tend to favour sources written in that language. A machine translation rarely matches how people in Hong Kong phrase a question in Traditional Chinese, so each version should be written for its readers and linked with hreflang tags.

How long does it take to see results?

It depends on how often search engines and AI tools revisit your site, and on your competition. Technical fixes can be picked up within weeks. Authority from directories, press and expert content builds over months, which is why the Authority service has a three-month minimum.

Which firms do you work with?

Established Hong Kong firms whose clients research carefully before they choose: law firms, medical and dental clinics, wealth and immigration advisers, B2B consultancies, and property and luxury businesses.

How much does it cost?
